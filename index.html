Our purpose
Whole of Society Resilience means that everyone, at every level of the system, works together to build a thriving, adaptive and sustainable society.

We champion human-centred resilience. That means putting people — their needs, behaviours, relationships, agency and lived experience — at the heart of every system.

It means helping people feel empowered to take action within their own circle of influence, while connecting research, practice, policy and lived experience to build resilience that is more human, more joined-up and more effective.

How we think about resilience

It includes, but goes way beyond, the more traditional understanding of resilience as preparedness for adversity and responding to shock events.

01

A mindset shift

Whole Society Resilience requires a radical shift in mindset from a reactive to a proactive stance — a collaborative approach that breaks down silos and empowers community-led resilience initiatives.

It involves empowering people to respond effectively and collaboratively to complex challenges. To make sense of the deluge of data and information in a way that is purposeful and leads towards greater human, societal and planetary wellbeing.

02

A whole system approach

A whole system approach, underpinned by (complex) systems thinking, that connects the human, personal and social world with the technical, political and environmental ‘wicked’ challenges facing societies and our planet.

The unique contribution of the Centre is to deliberately bring together such capabilities — including the measurement models and data that inform them — to support and enhance the work that emerges from this ‘silo-busting’ approach.

We do this through innovative, industry-led projects and leadership and learning programmes which explore, model and advance a whole of society approach to resilience at multiple levels. We are developing a digital infrastructure to capture, model and analyse data so it can be used effectively for leadership decision-making for whole of society and place-based resilience policies.

03

A shared understanding

We promote a shared understanding of our purpose, shocks, stresses, opportunities and interdependencies.

The Centre provides an independent hub that holds the creative tension between the differing and often competing values and lifecycles of research, policy, practice, commerce and digital innovation.

We procure and promote projects and programmes that contribute new knowledge and know-how to the whole of society, including the most disadvantaged.

Leadership & learning

The Resilient Leaders Programme

Building resilience requires a proactive, systems-focused leadership approach that empowers communities and organisations to take ownership. This programme develops the leadership capabilities and mindsets needed to embed resilience across the system and deliver lasting change.

Delivered by PA and Leading4Change, both accredited Resilient Leaders Consultants, for resilience professionals across central government, local government, business and the voluntary sector.

CWSR Membership Charter

What members agree to

As a Member of the Centre for Whole Society Resilience (CWSR) I agree to work collaboratively and inclusively with other members to forward the purpose of the Centre in delivering Whole Society Resilience. Specifically, I will endeavour to:

  1. participate in dialogue that promotes and enhances the centre on social media and at industry events;
  2. be willing to host a CWSR meeting if you have appropriate facilities;
  3. contribute to discussion forums, papers, articles or projects with other members.
