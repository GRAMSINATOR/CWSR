Save the Date: CWSR Members’ Day 2026

2 September 2026By Stefano Piscitelli1 min read

Thursday 15 October 2026

London · 10.00am to 4.00pm

CWSR Members’ Day returns this October, bringing together members from across sectors to explore this year’s theme of human-centred resilience.

The day will look at resilience across individuals, households, organisations and communities, and what this means for how we prepare, respond and work together.

Members can expect a mix of external perspectives, discussion and practical reflection, with plenty of opportunity to share experience, challenge thinking and help shape the future direction of CWSR.

We are currently confirming speakers and contributors, with the full programme to follow shortly.

Help shape the conversation

We want the day to reflect the priorities of our members. As part of registration, we are asking:

  • What issue or challenge relating to human-centred resilience would you most like Members’ Day to explore?
  • Is there an example, experience, person or piece of work that other CWSR members could learn from?

Your responses will help us shape the programme and focus the discussion on the issues that matter most.

Places are limited, so members are encouraged to register their interest here.
