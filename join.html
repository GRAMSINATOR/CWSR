Membership is free. If you care about resilience and have skills to offer, we'd like you to join us. Read the charter, then fill in the short sign-up form.

The members' charter

As a member of the Centre for Whole of Society Resilience, I agree to work collaboratively and inclusively with other members to further the Centre's purpose of delivering whole of society resilience. Specifically, I will endeavour to:

  1. participate in dialogue that promotes and enhances the Centre on social media and at industry events;
  2. be willing to host a CWSR meeting if I have appropriate facilities;
  3. contribute to discussion forums, papers, articles or projects with other members.
