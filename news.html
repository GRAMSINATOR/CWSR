CWSR Membership Charter

What members agree to

As a Member of the Centre for Whole Society Resilience (CWSR) I agree to work collaboratively and inclusively with other members to forward the purpose of the Centre in delivering Whole Society Resilience. Specifically, I will endeavour to:

  1. participate in dialogue that promotes and enhances the centre on social media and at industry events;
  2. be willing to host a CWSR meeting if you have appropriate facilities;
  3. contribute to discussion forums, papers, articles or projects with other members.

Questions? Contact us at connect@wholesocietyresilience.org
